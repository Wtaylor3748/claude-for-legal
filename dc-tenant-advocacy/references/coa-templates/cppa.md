# D.C. Consumer Protection Procedures Act (CPPA) — landlord-tenant

**Statute:** D.C. Code §§ 28-3901 to 28-3913. Unlawful trade practices: § 28-3904. Private action: § 28-3905(k)(1)(A). Landlord-tenant coverage: § 28-3905(k)(6).

## ⚠️ Threshold issue — coverage of landlord-tenant claims

- *Gomez v. Independence Mgmt. of Del., Inc.*, 967 A.2d 1276, 1286 (D.C. 2009) held the Council "did not intend . . . to extend the private right of action created by the CPPA into the realm of landlord-tenant relations." `[CourtListener — retrieved 2026-10-07]`. *Falconi-Sachs v. LPF Senate Square, LLC*, 142 A.3d 550 (D.C. 2016), applied *Gomez* to dismiss a tenant's CPPA claim. `[CourtListener — retrieved 2026-10-07]`
- **The Council reversed that in 2018.** At-Risk Tenant Protection Clarifying Amendment Act of 2018, D.C. Law 22-206, added § 28-3905(k)(6): the private right of action "shall apply to trade practices arising from landlord-tenant relations." Applied in *Hettinger v. Bozzuto Mgmt. Co.* (D.D.C. July 21, 2025), which called *Gomez* "misplaced" on this point and held that a Rental Housing Act violation in a consumer context is a per se CPPA violation. `[CourtListener — retrieved 2026-10-07]` *Hettinger* is a federal district court decision — persuasive, not binding, on D.C. courts.
- `[review]` **Timing.** Conduct before D.C. Law 22-206's effective date may still be argued under *Gomez*. Confirm the effective date on code.dccouncil.gov and whether any DCCA decision has addressed retroactivity. `[verify]`
- Opposing counsel citing *Gomez* without addressing § 28-3905(k)(6) is a point to hand to `counter-narrative`.

# Retaliatory action — D.C. Code § 42-3505.02

**Statute:** D.C. Code § 42-3505.02 (Rental Housing Act of 1985 § 502). `[verify current text and list of protected acts]`

**Presumption:** Landlord action within six months of the tenant's protected act is presumed retaliatory; the landlord must rebut it. *Borger Mgmt., Inc. v. Sindram*, 886 A.2d 52, 63 (D.C. 2005) ("under the statute, Borger's actions were presumed retaliatory due to the fact that they occurred within six months of Sindram's petition to the DCRA. . . . The only way that this presumption could be overcome was by Borger's establishing that its actions were not retaliatory."). `[CourtListener — retrieved 2026-10-07]` *Borger* also notes one rebuttal route is showing the action was "otherwise permitted by law." `[CourtListener]`. The statute's evidentiary standard for rebuttal `[verify — clear and convincing evidence per § 42-3505.02(b)]`.

## Common gaps to check

- Exact dates for both the protected act and the landlord action — the presumption turns on them.
- Proof the landlord knew of the protected act.
- Each separate protected act restarts a six-month window; chart all of them.

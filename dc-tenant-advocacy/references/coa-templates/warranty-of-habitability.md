# Breach of the implied warranty of habitability (D.C.)

**Core authority:** *Javins v. First Nat'l Realty Corp.*, 428 F.2d 1071 (D.C. Cir. 1970) `[CourtListener — retrieved 2026-10-07]`. The court held that the old no-repair rule "must be abandoned in favor of an implied warranty of habitability," and that "[i]n the District of Columbia, the standards of this warranty are set out in the Housing Regulations." *Id.* at 1076–77. Footnote 62: violations "must affect the tenant's apartment or common areas which the tenant uses."

**Housing standards:** 14 DCMR (Housing Code) `[model knowledge — verify current chapter/section numbers]`.

## Elements (tenant asserting)

| # | Element | Typical evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Landlord–tenant relationship under a residential lease | Lease, rent ledger, receipts | *Javins* `[CourtListener]` |
| 2 | A condition that violates the Housing Code (or renders the unit unfit) in the tenant's unit or common areas the tenant uses | DOB/DCRA inspection reports, notices of infraction, photos/video with dates, 311 records, expert or inspector testimony | *Javins* at 1077 & n.62 `[CourtListener]`; 14 DCMR `[verify]` |
| 3 | Landlord had notice of the condition (actual or constructive) | Written repair requests, emails, portal tickets, inspection notices served on landlord | `[model knowledge — verify]` — notice is generally required; confirm current DCCA formulation |
| 4 | Landlord failed to correct within a reasonable time | Timeline of requests vs. repairs (pull from `chronology`) | `[model knowledge — verify]` |
| 5 | Tenant did not cause the condition | Testimony; inspector findings | `[model knowledge — verify]` |
| 6 | Damages — reduced rental value for the period of breach (rent abatement), plus consequential damages where proven | Rent paid, percentage-reduction method, out-of-pocket costs → hand to `damages-calculator` | `[model knowledge — verify]` |

## Uses

- **Defense in a nonpayment case:** rent owed is reduced by the abatement; can bring rent owed to zero.
- **Counterclaim:** abatement for past periods actually paid, subject to limitations period `[verify]`.
- **Protective-order hearings:** habitability evidence is a basis to reduce the protective-order amount `[verify — McNeal v. Habib, 346 A.2d 508 (D.C. 1975)]`.

## Common gaps to check

- Dated proof of *notice* to the landlord for each condition.
- Proof the condition existed for each month claimed (not just on inspection day).
- A defensible abatement methodology (percentage of rent per condition per month).
